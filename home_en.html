Datasets that include unusual objects and scenarios are essential for developing robust and safe autonomous driving systems. Multisensor data play a key role by enabling algorithms to accurately perceive and interpret diverse environmental conditions. By combining information from cameras, depth sensors, LiDAR, and radar, the systems achieve a more complete and reliable understanding of their surroundings. This improves decision-making and enhances the vehicle’s ability to detect and safely respond to unusual or rare situations. The result is greater reliability and safety of autonomous vehicles in real-world, complex traffic environments.

At the Mathematical Modelling of Industrial Life Cycles research group at the Institute of Mathematics, TU Berlin, we are developing such a dataset, which will be made publicly available to advance research and industry in Germany and worldwide. This dataset will enable researchers and developers to test and improve their algorithms under realistic conditions, contributing to the development of safer and more reliable autonomous driving systems.
